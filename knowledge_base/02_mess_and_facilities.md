# Green Valley Students' Hostel — Mess and Facilities Guide

> **Provenance:** This document was written from scratch for the Hostel Helpdesk capstone
> project. It is fictional and does not describe any real hostel, person, or institution.

**Document owner:** Mess Committee and Facilities Desk
**Applies to:** All residents of Block A, Block B, and Block C

---

## 1. The mess at a glance

The Hostel mess is a shared dining hall on the ground floor of Block A. It serves vegetarian
and non-vegetarian options at fixed times. The mess is run by the Hostel on a no-profit basis,
and the menu rotates on a weekly cycle so residents know what to expect.

**Mess timings (all days):**

| Meal | Serving window |
| --- | --- |
| Breakfast | 07:00 – 09:00 |
| Lunch | 12:30 – 14:00 |
| Evening snacks | 17:00 – 18:00 |
| Dinner | 19:30 – 21:30 |

Residents must arrive within the serving window. The counter closes sharply at the end of each
window. Anyone with a class or lab that clashes with a meal window should speak to the Mess
Supervisor about a late plate, which must be requested at least two hours in advance.

## 3. Guest meals and extra meals

1. Registered residents eat as part of the monthly mess fee.
2. A **guest meal** lets a resident's visitor eat one meal in the mess with them. The guest
   must be signed in at the visitor lounge first.
3. Each guest meal is charged at a flat rate. **The current guest meal rate is ₹150 per
   person per meal.** Children below five years are not charged.
4. Guest meal tokens can be bought at the Hostel Office or from the Mess Supervisor at least
   **two hours before** the meal window.
5. Extra meals for a resident (beyond the standard three meals and snacks) are also charged at
   the guest meal rate of ₹150.
6. Guest meals are added to the resident's monthly bill. They are not deducted from the mess
   fee, and they are not refundable once the meal has been served.

---

## 4. Special diets and sick meals

1. Residents with a medical dietary requirement (for example, food allergies or a doctor's
   advised diet) should register the requirement with the Mess Supervisor and the Warden. A
   doctor's note may be requested.
2. A resident who is unwell and cannot come to the mess may request a **sick plate** to be
   sent to their room. This must be arranged through the Mess Supervisor, ideally before the
   serving window.
3. Fasting days observed by residents can be accommodated if the resident informs the mess one
   day in advance.

---

## 5. Mess complaints and hygiene

1. Any complaint about food quality, hygiene, or an insect found in food must be reported to
   the Mess Supervisor immediately, and also logged through the complaint system.
2. A **Mess Committee** of resident representatives meets the Mess Supervisor once a week to
   review feedback.
3. Food is not allowed to be removed from the mess hall except sick plates and pre-approved
   late plates.

---

## 6. Laundry

1. The Hostel runs a shared laundry in the basement of Block B.
2. **Laundry pickup days are Tuesday and Friday.** Residents drop their bag before 10:00 on a
   pickup day and collect it the following day after 17:00.
3. Each resident is allowed one wash load per week as part of the hostel service. Additional
   loads are paid per load at the Hostel Office.
4. The Hostel is not responsible for damage to delicate garments. Residents should wash
   delicate items by hand.
5. Self-service washing machines are available in the basement laundry and can be booked
   through the laundry register on a first-come, first-served basis.

---

## 7. Wi-Fi and internet

1. The Hostel provides Wi-Fi in all rooms and common areas.
2. Each resident receives one login, valid for **two devices**. More devices require approval
   from the Hostel Office.
3. Wi-Fi is for academic and personal use. The following are not permitted over the Hostel
   network: torrenting, illegal downloads, online gambling, and running any kind of server or
   public hotspot.
4. Streaming of video is allowed within a fair-use limit. During peak hours (20:00 – 23:00),
   heavy downloads may be throttled so that everyone gets a workable connection.
5. Wi-Fi problems should be logged as an **Internet** category complaint. Simple fixes
   (restart, re-login) should be tried first.

---

## 8. Common room and recreation

1. The common room, on the ground floor of Block A, is open from **06:00 to 23:00**.
2. The television in the common room is shared. Residents should agree on what to watch and
   keep the volume reasonable.
3. Board games and a small indoor-games corner (carrom, chess, table tennis) are available in
   the common room on a first-come basis.
4. Reservation of the common room for a group event requires Warden approval.

---

## 9. Gym and sports room

1. The small gym is in the basement of Block C and is open **06:00 – 09:00** and
   **17:00 – 21:00**.
2. Residents must wipe down equipment after use and wear appropriate footwear.
3. The gym is used at the resident's own risk. Residents with a medical condition should
   consult a doctor before using the gym.
4. Sports equipment must be returned to its place after use.

---

## 10. Study hall and library nook

1. The Study Hall on the first floor of Block B is open **24 hours**.
2. Silent study rules apply at all times in the Study Hall — no calls, no music, no group
   discussion.
3. A small library nook has reference books donated by seniors. Books must be used within the
   hall and may not be taken to rooms.
4. Lighting and fans in the Study Hall are on the backup power circuit so they stay on during
   a power cut.

---

## 11. Water, power and other facilities

1. **Drinking water:** RO water purifiers are on every floor. Residents must not store open
   water containers in corridors.
2. **Hot water:** Geysers run **06:00 – 09:00** and **18:00 – 21:00**. Please do not leave a
   geyser switched on outside these windows.
3. **Power backup:** A generator/inverter backs up corridor lights, the Study Hall, and one
   socket per room. Heavy appliances are not supported on backup.
4. **Ironing point:** A shared ironing point is provided on each floor. Personal irons may not
   be used inside rooms.
5. **Vending:** A snack vending machine is near the main gate. The Hostel is not responsible
   for a vending machine malfunction — report it at the Hostel Office.
6. **Notice board:** Official notices are posted on the board near the mess entrance. Residents
   are expected to check it at least twice a week.
