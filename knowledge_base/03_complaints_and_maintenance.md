# Green Valley Students' Hostel — Complaints and Maintenance Process

> **Provenance:** This document was written from scratch for the Hostel Helpdesk capstone
> project. It is fictional and does not describe any real hostel, person, or institution.

**Document owner:** Maintenance Desk and Warden's Office
**Applies to:** All residents of Block A, Block B, and Block C

---

## 1. Purpose

This document explains how a resident raises a complaint, how long a fix normally takes, and
how to escalate if something is not getting done. Following this process means your complaint
is logged with a ticket number and cannot be lost.

---

## 2. How to raise a complaint

There are three ways to raise a complaint:

1. **Online complaint form** — the fastest route. Open the Hostel complaint form, choose a
   category, describe the problem, and mention your room number.
2. **Hostel Office desk** — for residents who prefer to report in person during office hours.
3. **Phone / security desk** — only for urgent problems outside office hours (for example, a
   major leak or a power failure at night).

Every complaint is given a **ticket number**. Keep it — you will need it to track or escalate.

### What to include

A good complaint includes:

- Your **room number** and block (for example, "B-214").
- The **category** (see section 3).
- A short, clear description of the problem.
- Whether it is **urgent** (see section 5).
- A contact number or your Hostel ID.

| Category | Examples | Handled by |
| --- | --- | --- |

| Plumbing | Leaking tap, blocked drain, no water | Plumbing team |
| Carpentry | Broken door, loose bed, damaged cupboard | Carpentry team |

## 4. Service level — how long a fix normally takes

The table below shows the **target** resolution time, measured from the time the complaint is
logged. These are targets on normal working days; weekends and holidays may add a day.

| Priority | Description | Target resolution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Urgent | Safety risk or something that makes the room unusable | Within 4 hours |
| High | Major inconvenience (no water, no power to a room) | Within 24 hours |
| Normal | Everyday repair (fan, tap, furniture) | Within 3 working days |
| Low | Cosmetic or non-urgent improvement | Within 7 working days |

A complaint is "resolved" only when the resident confirms the fix. If you are not satisfied,
you may reopen the complaint with the same ticket number within **48 hours**.

---

## 5. What counts as urgent

Call the security desk immediately, in addition to logging the complaint, if you see any of
the following:

- Sparking socket, smoke, or a burning smell.
- A major water leak or flooding.
- A fire or suspected fire.
- A broken railing or a structural safety risk.
- No power or no water affecting a whole floor.
- Any situation that threatens the safety of a person.

For life-threatening emergencies, always call the emergency numbers in the
**"Emergency and Contacts"** document first.

---

## 6. Escalation ladder

If your complaint is not resolved within the target time, escalate in this order:

1. **Level 1 — Reopen with the ticket number.** Reply to the complaint with the same ticket.
   This reminds the assigned team.
2. **Level 2 — Maintenance Supervisor.** If still not resolved after one more day, ask the
   Maintenance Supervisor to review the ticket.
3. **Level 3 — Warden.** If the problem affects safety or has been open for more than a week,
   take it to the Warden.
4. **Level 4 — Grievance Committee.** For unresolved or serious matters, the Hostel Grievance
   Committee reviews the case and records a decision.

Escalating a complaint twice within the same day without waiting for the target time is not
encouraged; it slows down everyone's tickets.

---

## 7. Recurring problems

If the same fault keeps returning (for example, a tap that has been "fixed" three times),
mention the previous ticket numbers. A repeated fault is treated as a **replacement** issue
rather than a repair, and is sent to the Maintenance Supervisor directly.

---

## 8. Damage caused by a resident

1. Repairs needed because of misuse or damage by a resident are chargeable to that resident.
2. The Maintenance team will record the damage and note the estimated cost. The cost is added
   to the resident's next bill or recovered from the deposit.
3. Residents must not attempt their own repairs. A repair attempted by a resident that causes
   further damage will be charged in full.

---

## 9. Feedback and quality

1. After a ticket is closed, the resident may rate the fix (good / average / poor).
2. Ratings are reviewed by the Maintenance Supervisor every week.
3. Repeated poor ratings on a category are escalated to the Warden.

---

## 10. What the complaint system does **not** handle

The complaint system is only for physical hostel problems and mess issues. It does **not**
handle:

- Changing rooms (this is a Warden approval matter).
- Fee or deposit disputes (take these to the Hostel Office accountant).
- Disciplinary or ragging matters (take these to the Warden or Grievance Committee).
- Anything outside the Hostel (for example, a problem in a classroom or a different building).

If you send one of these through the complaint form, it will be forwarded to the right desk,
but it is faster to go straight to the correct place.
