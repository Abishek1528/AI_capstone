# Green Valley Students' Hostel — Rules and Regulations

> **Provenance:** This document was written from scratch for the Hostel Helpdesk capstone
> project. It is fictional and does not describe any real hostel, person, or institution.

**Document owner:** Hostel Office, Green Valley Students' Hostel
**Version:** 4.2 (revised this academic year)
**Applies to:** All residents of Block A, Block B, and Block C

---

## 1. About this handbook

Green Valley Students' Hostel ("the Hostel") provides accommodation for up to 320 students
across three blocks. This handbook explains the everyday rules that keep the Hostel safe,
quiet and fair for everyone. It is written for residents, but it also applies to any guest a
resident brings in.

Where this handbook and a notice on the Hostel board disagree, the newer published notice
wins. Where a rule refers to money, the exact amounts live in the separate document
**"Fees, Deposit and Fines"** — this handbook only tells you that a fine exists, not how much.

Three words are used consistently throughout:

- **Resident** — a student with a confirmed room allotment.
- **Warden** — the staff member on duty who is responsible for discipline and emergencies.
- **Hostel Office** — the administrative desk near the main gate, open on working days.

---

## 2. Eligibility and admission

1. Accommodation is open to students enrolled in a recognised full-time programme.
2. A room is confirmed only after the admission form is signed, the security deposit is paid
   in full, and the Hostel Office issues a **room allotment slip**.
3. Allotment is valid for one academic year and must be renewed before the renewal deadline
   announced each April.
4. Residents must keep their enrolment current. If a resident is no longer an enrolled
   student, the allotment becomes void and the resident must vacate within seven days of the
   Hostel Office notice.

---

## 3. Room allotment and occupancy

1. Rooms are allotted by the Hostel Office. Residents may not swap rooms privately; all
   changes must be approved by the Warden.
2. Single, double and triple occupancy rooms exist. A resident must occupy only the bed
   allotted to them.
3. Residents are responsible for the furniture and fittings listed in their room inventory
   at the time of joining. Damage beyond normal wear is chargeable.
4. Empty beds may be filled by the Hostel Office at any time; residents cannot reserve a bed
   for a friend.
5. Personal belongings must stay within the resident's own room. Corridors and staircases
   must be kept clear at all times.

---

## 4. Entry, exit and gate timings

1. The main gate closes at **22:30** on all days. Residents arriving after 22:30 must sign the
   **late-entry register** at the security desk and give a reason.
2. Residents must carry their Hostel identity card at all times inside the campus and show it
   on demand to security staff.
3. Night-out (staying away overnight) requires prior approval through the night-out request
   form, submitted to the Warden by **18:00** on the day of departure.
4. Residents going home for a vacation or a leave longer than three days must inform the
   Warden in writing and sign out at the security desk.
5. During emergency drills or a declared emergency, gate timings do not apply and residents
   must follow security instructions.

---

## 5. Quiet hours and study environment

1. **Quiet hours** are **22:00 to 06:00** on all days. During quiet hours, residents must not
   play music, use loudspeakers, or hold loud conversations in corridors.
2. **Silent study hours** apply in the Study Hall at all times. Talking on phones inside the
   Study Hall is not permitted.
3. Personal music and calls are allowed in rooms during the day, but volume must not disturb
   neighbours. Repeated complaints about noise are treated as a discipline matter.
4. The common room is the only place where group entertainment is allowed after 22:00, and
   even there the volume must stay reasonable.

---

## 6. Guests and visitors

1. Visitors are allowed in the **visitor lounge** near the main gate between **09:00 and
   19:00**. Visitors are not allowed inside residential corridors or rooms.
2. All visitors must sign the visitor register. Residents must accompany their visitor at all
   times and are responsible for their visitor's behaviour.
3. **Overnight guests are not allowed**, under any circumstances. This applies to family
   members as well.
4. Guest meals may be arranged at the mess for a small charge; see the mess document for the
   current per-meal rate.
5. Anyone found bringing an unauthorised person into a room overnight will face disciplinary
   action, including a fine and possible cancellation of the allotment.

---

## 7. Prohibited items and activities

The following are strictly prohibited inside the Hostel:

1. Alcohol, tobacco, vaping products, and any non-prescribed intoxicants.
2. Any cooking appliance inside rooms, including induction cooktops, electric kettles,
   immersion rods, and rice cookers. (A shared pantry is provided on each floor.)
3. Heaters, irons used outside the designated ironing point, and any high-wattage appliance
   not approved by the Hostel Office.
4. Firearms, sharp weapons, firecrackers, and flammable liquids.
5. Pets of any kind.
6. Posters, painting, or drilling into walls without the Warden's permission.
7. Gambling, betting, and any activity prohibited by law.
8. Use of the Hostel Wi-Fi for illegal downloads, torrenting, or running servers.

Possession of a prohibited item is itself a violation, even if the item is not in use.

---

## 8. Cleanliness and housekeeping

1. Housekeeping staff clean corridors, bathrooms, and common areas daily. **Rooms are not
   cleaned by staff** — residents keep their own rooms clean.
2. Residents must keep waste in the bins provided and use the separate wet and dry bins on
   each floor.
3. Food waste must not be left in rooms overnight; it should be placed in the corridor bin.
4. Bathrooms shared within a room are cleaned by rotation by the room's residents.
5. A resident who repeatedly leaves their area dirty may be fined, and housekeeping
   complaints are logged through the standard complaint process.

---

## 9. Electrical safety and appliances

1. Extension boards must be of ISI-marked quality and must not be daisy-chained.
2. If a socket sparks, or a light or fan stops working, report it immediately through the
   complaint system. Do not attempt repairs yourself.
3. Residents must switch off lights, fans and chargers when leaving the room.
4. The Hostel is not responsible for loss of or damage to personal electronics. Residents are
   strongly advised to keep valuable items locked.
5. Power backup runs on the corridor lights and one socket per room. Backup power is for
   lighting and charging only, not for heavy appliances.

---

## 10. Ragging, harassment and discipline

1. Ragging in any form is **strictly forbidden** and is a serious offence. Any complaint of
   ragging will be referred to the Hostel Grievance Committee and the institution's
   anti-ragging cell.
2. Harassment, bullying, discrimination, or intimidation of any resident is forbidden.
3. Complaints of a serious nature may be made confidentially to the Warden or through the
   Grievance Committee. Complainants' identities are protected.
4. The Warden may immediately suspend a resident from the Hostel pending an enquiry where
   there is a credible safety risk.
5. Disciplinary outcomes range from a written warning, to a fine, to cancellation of
   allotment, depending on severity.

---

## 11. Bicycle and vehicle parking

1. Bicycles must be parked only in the bicycle stand behind Block B. Bicycles may not be
   taken into rooms or corridors.
2. Two-wheelers require a parking sticker from the Hostel Office and must be parked in the
   two-wheeler bay. Four-wheelers are generally not permitted for residents.
3. The Hostel provides parking space as a courtesy and is not responsible for theft or damage.

---

## 12. Leave, absence and vacation

1. For any absence of more than three nights, a resident must file a leave request with the
   Warden and record it in the leave register.
2. During semester breaks, residents must either vacate or register to stay, as announced.
   Rooms are not guaranteed to be held if a resident does not register.
3. Leaving the Hostel for an extended period without informing the Warden is treated as an
   unexplained absence and may attract a fine.
4. Residents must carry home their personal belongings during long vacations; the Hostel is
   not responsible for items left in a locked room.

---

## 13. Penalties summary

The Hostel issues fines for rule violations. The **specific amounts** are defined in the
document **"Fees, Deposit and Fines"**. In general, fines apply to:

- Late payment of hostel fees.
- Late entry without signing the register.
- Unauthorised overnight guests.
- Possession of prohibited items.
- Damage to hostel property.
- Repeated noise or cleanliness complaints.

Fines are added to the resident's next monthly bill, or deducted from the security deposit at
the time of vacating if unpaid.
