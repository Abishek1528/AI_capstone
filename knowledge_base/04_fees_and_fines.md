# Green Valley Students' Hostel — Fees, Deposit and Fines

> **Provenance:** This document was written from scratch for the Hostel Helpdesk capstone
> project. It is fictional and does not describe any real hostel, person, or institution.

**Document owner:** Hostel Office (Accounts)
**Applies to:** All residents of Block A, Block B, and Block C
**This version supersedes all earlier fee circulars.**

---

## 1. Monthly fee structure

Every resident pays the following each month:

| Item | Amount (₹ per month) | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Room rent (triple sharing) | 4,500 | Standard rate |
| Room rent (double sharing) | 5,800 | Where available |
| Room rent (single) | 7,500 | Limited rooms |
| Mess fee | 3,000 | Three meals and evening snacks |
| Wi-Fi and utilities | 350 | Included in most bills |

A resident pays the rent for their allotted room type plus the mess fee and utilities. The
figures above are for the current academic year and may be revised at the start of a new year
with one month's notice.

---

## 2. Security deposit

1. A **refundable security deposit of ₹10,000** is paid at the time of admission.
2. The deposit is held for the duration of the residency and is refunded at the time of
   vacating, after deductions for dues, fines, and any damage.
3. The deposit is **not** adjusted against monthly rent while the resident is living in the
   Hostel.
4. Refunds are processed within **21 working days** of vacating and clearing all dues.

---

## 3. Due dates

1. Monthly dues must be paid **on or before the 5th of the month** for which they are billed.
2. The bill is generated on the 1st of each month and is available from the Hostel Office.
3. If the 5th falls on a holiday, the due date moves to the next working day.
4. Residents who vacate mid-month are billed pro-rata for the days occupied, rounded up to a
   full day.

---

## 4. Late fee policy (important)

If a monthly payment is not received by the due date, a **late fee** is added. The late fee is
calculated on the number of days a payment is late:

- **Days 1 to 5 late:** ₹50 per day.
- **Day 6 and every day after:** ₹100 per day.
- **Maximum late fee per late payment: ₹2,000.** The late fee never exceeds ₹2,000, no matter
  how many days late the payment is.

### Worked examples

**Example A — 3 days late**

- Days 1–3 are in the ₹50/day band → 3 × ₹50 = **₹150**.

**Example B — 5 days late**

- Days 1–5 are in the ₹50/day band → 5 × ₹50 = **₹250**.

**Example C — 8 days late**

- Days 1–5: 5 × ₹50 = ₹250.
- Days 6–8: 3 × ₹100 = ₹300.
- Total = **₹550**.

**Example D — 25 days late**

- Days 1–5: 5 × ₹50 = ₹250.
- Days 6–25: 20 × ₹100 = ₹2,000.
- Sub-total = ₹2,250, but the cap applies → **₹2,000**.

The late fee is added to the resident's next bill. Writing to the Hostel Office explaining a
genuine delay (for example, a bank failure) may result in a partial waiver at the Warden's
discretion, but the late fee is otherwise automatic.

---

## 5. Fine schedule for rule violations

Separate from late fees, the following fines apply to rule violations described in the
**"Rules and Regulations"** document:

| Violation | Fine (₹) |
| --- | --- |
| Late entry without signing the register | 100 |
| Unauthorised overnight guest | 1,000 |
| Possession of a prohibited appliance (kettle, heater, etc.) | 500 |
| Possession of alcohol or tobacco | 1,500 |
| Damaging hostel property | Actual repair cost |
| Repeated noise complaints (third time onward) | 200 |
| Not clearing waste / hygiene violation (repeated) | 150 |
| Unexplained extended absence without informing the Warden | 300 |

Fines are decided by the Warden and are added to the next monthly bill or recovered from the
deposit.

---

## 6. Guest meal charges

Guest meals are not part of the monthly mess fee. Each guest meal is charged at **₹150 per
person per meal** and is added to the resident's bill. See the mess guide for the rules on
arranging a guest meal.

---

## 7. Payment methods

1. **Online transfer** to the Hostel account — the preferred method. Use your Hostel ID as the
   payment reference.
2. **UPI** to the Hostel UPI ID shown on the bill.
3. **Debit card** at the Hostel Office during office hours.
4. Cash is accepted only at the Hostel Office desk and only with an official receipt.

Always keep the receipt or payment confirmation. A payment is considered made only when the
Hostel Office has recorded it.

---

## 8. Refunds

1. Mess fee already paid is not refunded for days a resident chooses not to eat.
2. If a resident vacates mid-month with the Warden's approval, rent for unused full days may be
   adjusted at the Hostel Office's discretion after deducting one month's notice.
3. A resident leaving without one month's written notice forfeits one month's rent from the
   deposit.
4. All refunds are made to the bank account registered with the Hostel Office.

---

## 9. Disputes

1. Any dispute about a bill must be raised with the Hostel Office within **15 days** of the
   bill date.
2. Unresolved disputes go to the Warden, and then to the Grievance Committee.
3. Amounts under dispute must still be paid, and any overcharge is refunded once the dispute
   is settled.
