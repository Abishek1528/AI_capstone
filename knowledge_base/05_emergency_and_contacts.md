## 2. Fire safety

1. If you discover a fire, **raise the alarm first** — shout "fire" and press the nearest fire
   alarm point.
2. Call the fire service and the security desk.
3. Leave the building using the **stairs**. Never use the lift during a fire.
4. Close your room door behind you if it is safe to do so, to slow the spread of smoke.
5. Assemble at the designated assembly point: the **open ground near the main gate**.
6. Do not re-enter the building until security or the fire service says it is safe.
7. Fire extinguishers are placed at each floor's staircase landing. Residents should know
   where the nearest one is — but only use an extinguisher on a small, contained fire.
8. Fire drills are held every semester and are compulsory. Blocking a corridor or staircase
   with belongings is a safety violation and attracts a fine.

## 3. Medical emergencies

1. For a serious injury or collapse, call the **ambulance (108)** and the **security desk**,
   and send someone to guide the ambulance to the right block.
2. The campus health centre treats minor illnesses and injuries. Its number is on the notice
   board and on the Hostel app/board.
3. The Hostel keeps a **first-aid box** at the security desk. It is for immediate first aid,
   not for routine medication.
4. Residents with a chronic condition (for example, asthma, diabetes, or a severe allergy)
   should register it, in confidence, with the Warden so staff can help in an emergency.
5. Any medicine storage that needs refrigeration should be arranged with the Warden in advance.
6. **The Hostel does not give medical advice.** For any question about medicine, symptoms, or
   treatment, consult a qualified doctor or the campus health centre. Hostel staff will direct
   you to medical help, nothing more.

---

## 4. Security incidents

1. If you see an intruder, theft, or any threatening behaviour, do not confront the person.
   Move to safety and call the **security desk** and, if needed, the **police (100)**.
2. Report lost keys to the security desk immediately, so a lock can be changed if required.
3. For theft, file a written complaint with the Warden as well as a police complaint where
   appropriate.
4. Keep your room door locked when you leave, even for a short time.
5. The Hostel is not liable for loss of personal property, but all incidents should still be
   reported so they can be investigated.

---

## 5. Electrical and gas safety

1. Never touch an electrical point with wet hands.
2. If you smell burning or see smoke from a socket, switch off the mains for that room if it is
   safe, and call the security desk immediately.
3. Do not overload sockets or use damaged extension boards.
4. Cooking appliances are not allowed in rooms (see the rules document). Cooking is only
   allowed in the shared pantry.
5. If a gas smell is noticed in or near a pantry, do not switch any electrical switch on or
   off; open windows, leave the area, and alert security.

## 8. Emergency checklist — what to do in the first minute

1. **Stay calm.** Take a breath.
2. **Raise the alarm** so others know.
3. **Call the right number** — security desk for anything on campus, 108 / 101 / 100 for
   medical, fire, or police.
4. **Move to safety** and help others only if it is safe for you to do so.
5. **Follow security instructions** at the assembly point.
6. **Do not re-enter** until cleared.
7. **Report afterwards** to the Warden so the incident is recorded.

---

## 9. Grievance and support

1. Ragging and harassment complaints are handled confidentially by the Grievance Committee and
   the institution's anti-ragging cell.
2. Mental-health or wellbeing concerns can be raised with the Warden, who can connect a
   resident with the campus counsellor. The Hostel treats such matters with confidentiality.
3. Matters that are not emergencies should go through the normal complaint or request process
   rather than the emergency line.
